_Socials: nah

Questions about OLS

What do you do at OLS? What does your day look like?

Breakfast and coffee at a leisurely pace with my book, please! A meeting at 10 AM is too early, honestly. I like having a slower start to my day. As co-executive director, my day tends to change a lot from week to week. Some constants are 1:1s with my direct reports (that’s Laura Carter, Riva Quiroga, and Irene Ramos), catchups with my co-exec Patricia Herterich, and weekly team catchups. I use reclaim.ai to help schedule breaks between meetings and enforce my calendar having lunchtimes in it, as well as using it to mirror between my personal calendar and work calendar. The other more varied things involve spending time networking with community, funders, clients, and others in the open research ecosystem, and reading, writing, reflecting. We build a lot of reflection time into our work as a team, with (roughly) quarterly 1:1 retrospectives, as well as project-specific retros. This helps us to learn from things that didn’t go well, so we can iterate and try new things - but also allows us to identify things that do go well, so we can be proud of ourselves and continue doing those bits. This gives us the space to take “failures” and make them “learnings” instead - a much more pleasant way to go through life.

What are your interests besides your OLS work?

Oh no, are you accusing me of having a life outside my job?! Just kidding ;) I love nature, and physical activities. My teenage self would find that hilarious as I did everything I could to get out of school physical activity lessons (known as “sport” lessons in Israel). The best way to make me “recharge” is to put me somewhere where there are more plants than humans for a few hours. In the past I’ve cycled long distance (UK Coast-to-Coast, other general bikepacking), run half-marathons, and done long distance hikes, most recently a week walking around the island of Jersey.

What would you like people to stop asking you about?

My pronouns. I don’t associate strongly with any gender and I hate how much people expect me to have a ‘standard’ gender identity. I wish the languages I spoke didn’t have gendered pronouns at all.

What mythical creature would you believe as real?

A Companion. Every time I see a white horse I will it to Choose me, but they usually just ignore me. I guess my mindspeech isn’t strong enough.

What is the strangest thing that you have seen while walking down the street?

In Glastonbury in 2014, I was corralled into participating in a show of some sort. We only communicated via mime, and I had to wear a silver swimsuit over my clothes, and sing certain notes on-cue.

What is your guilty pleasure?

Eating a whole tub of Ben & Jerry’s Cookies on Cookie Dough icecream in one go. I exercise a lot for my mental and physical wellbeing, and the ability to eat a lot is a nice side-effect.

Garlic pasta with lots of olive oil and not much else.

A good pun or a poo joke.

DEEPER ONES

What is the meaning of life?

What we make of it, I reckon :) - for me that’s having a reasonably good time myself and trying to share the good times with others around me.

What gets you out of bed?

I know this is supposed to be a “deep” question but I really want to answer “either my bladder or a cat asking for breakfast”. I really enjoy lying in bed when it’s warm and snuggly, and maybe conning my spouse into getting me a coffee while I’m still in bed.

What do you wish you learned sooner?

I was forty years old when I learned that I was Autistic and had ADHD. It literally wasn’t possible to have been diagnosed with both when I was a child, as those two diagnoses used to be mutually exclusive. But damn, knowing I had a medically treatable condition (ADHD) and that I wasn’t just “naughty” or “a dreamer”…. That feels like I would have made one hell of a difference to my self esteem.

What do you like about society?

We, as humans, make speculative fiction. The thing about these books - often scifi or fantasy - is that they look at the world, say “what if this thing was different?” Then they take that difference and explore what society might be like. If volcanic eruptions were common, how might humanity survive? Can capitalism continue to thrive if there are light-years of distance between the enclaves of sentient beings? What might a whole world united under a single government look like, if it implemented universal basic income?
